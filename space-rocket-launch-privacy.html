This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Space Rocket Launch.

1. Game Features and Local Data

Space Rocket Launch does not require an account or login and does not provide in-app purchases, cloud saves, online leaderboards, or payment features.

Game progress, settings, blueprints, and similar gameplay data are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this local gameplay data to our own servers.

2. Advertising and Information Processed by Google

The game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. It also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent where required.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address;
  • app interactions, ad impressions, ad clicks, and rewarded-ad activity;
  • device, operating system, app version, language, and other technical information; and
  • diagnostic, crash, and performance information.

We do not directly store this advertising information on our own servers. Google processes this information under its own policies.

3. How Information Is Used

Advertising-related information may be used to show and measure ads, provide optional in-game rewards after a rewarded ad, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud and invalid traffic, comply with legal requirements, and improve service reliability.

We do not sell personal information and do not use advertising information to create our own user profiles.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, consent management, and compliance purposes.

Learn more at:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Your Choices

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's policies, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, the game displays Google's consent message. If Google indicates that privacy options are required, you can reopen them from the game's Settings menu by selecting Privacy Choices.

You can also manage advertising settings through your Android device settings or Google My Ad Center.

Rewarded video ads are optional. If you choose not to watch one, you will not receive that optional reward, but you can continue playing.

6. Children's Privacy

Space Rocket Launch is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a user account database or directly retain personal information for this game on our own servers. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and applicable law.

You can remove locally stored gameplay data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. This does not control information already processed by Google; use Google's privacy and advertising controls for those choices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the game's data handling to the features described in this policy. Information processed by Google is protected according to Google's security practices and policies.

9. International Processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is governed by their policies and applicable legal safeguards.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its service providers, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
