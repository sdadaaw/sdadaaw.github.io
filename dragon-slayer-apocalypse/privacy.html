This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Dragon Slayer: Apocalypse (the "Game").

1. Game Accounts, Purchases, and Developer Servers

The Game does not require you to create an account or log in. It does not offer in-app purchases, subscriptions, cloud saves, online leaderboards, or developer-operated account services.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your game progress. Game progress, settings, and related gameplay records are saved using storage on your device. Android or Google system backup features may handle app data according to your device and Google account settings; those platform features are not operated by us.

2. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, precise location, payment information, or government-issued identifiers through the Game.

We do not sell personal information and do not create our own advertising or user profile database.

3. Advertising and Information Processed by Google

The Game uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements, including optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect, receive, process, or share certain information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service operation.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google configuration, this information may include:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Interactions with the Game and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, ad views, video views, and ad clicks.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app or SDK launch time, hangs, energy usage, and technical errors.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers made available by the device or Google services.

We do not receive or store this advertising information on our own servers. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

4. How Advertising Information Is Used

Advertising information may be used to display and measure ads, provide in-game rewards after a rewarded ad is completed, limit repeated ads, prevent invalid traffic and abuse, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, and improve advertising services.

Rewarded video ads are optional. If you choose not to watch a rewarded video ad, you will not receive the related optional in-game reward, but you can continue using the Game.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices where required. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, age-related treatment, and Google's policies.

Where a privacy options entry point is required, you can reopen it from the Game's settings menu to review or change your choices. You may also manage or delete your Android advertising ID and adjust ad personalization settings through your device or Google settings where those controls are available.

6. Third-Party Services

Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. You can learn more at:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • My Ad Center and advertising choices

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to us.

Users who are below the age at which they can independently consent to data processing in their country should use the Game only with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian. Advertising requests may receive age-restricted treatment when required by applicable law, platform policy, or the information available to Google.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us at linxiansheng123@proton.me.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not operate user accounts or a game-data server, we do not retain your game progress on our own systems. You can remove locally stored game progress and settings by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game, subject to any Android or Google backup behavior enabled on your device.

Information processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and your available Google account, device, advertising, and consent controls.

9. Data Security and International Processing

We do not maintain a user account database for the Game. Information handled by Google may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, subject to Google's safeguards, policies, and applicable law.
